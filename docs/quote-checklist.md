# Questions to ask before comparing a flu shot quote

Use this checklist when calling or checking a provider's official booking page. Record what you are told and when you checked it. A published chain reference may have different conditions from a quote for one location.

## Record the location and payment basis

- Which exact location is quoting the price?
- Is this a pharmacy, a clinic inside a store, or another service? Do their fees differ?
- Is the amount a cash price, insurance copay, member price or voucher price?
- When does this quote expire, and how can I confirm it before visiting?

In the workbook, put the provider and location in the first input row, record the quote source or contact, and add the date checked. Avoid storing insurance IDs or other private identifiers in a shared file.

## Confirm the product and person

- What is the exact vaccine product name?
- Does this location serve this person's age group?
- Has the provider confirmed the number of remaining doses for this person?
- Does the quote cover each required dose, or just one?
- Is the product available at this location, and is an appointment required?

Use the provider's answers. The worksheet does not determine which vaccine or schedule is appropriate for an adult or child.

## Confirm the complete charge

- Is the price per dose or for the entire visit?
- Are administration, office-visit or other charges included?
- If fees are additional, are they charged per dose, per person, per visit or once for the group?
- Does the price require membership, a coupon, a particular payment method or another condition?
- Is any discount already included in the quoted price?

Convert any extra fees into a total for that option before entering them. For example, an invented $10 charge per person for two people becomes $20 in the total extra-fees field. Do not multiply a group fee again for each dose.

Only subtract an immediate discount that has not already been included in the quote. A future store voucher or reward is not an immediate reduction in the vaccination bill.

## Compare the same scope

Check that each option covers the same people and confirmed remaining doses. Ask the provider about any product differences. Use the same decision about including travel for all options, and total travel across every planned visit. Leave a cost blank when it is still unknown.
