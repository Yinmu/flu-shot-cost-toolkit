# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-08

- Published an original XLSX comparison workbook for up to three options and four people, with editable inputs and full arithmetic.
- Added a blank CSV quote collection sheet, a fictional household example and a provider-question checklist.
- Added visible links to the official website, https://flushotsnearme.com/, and its flu shot cost guide.
- Unknown required costs remain unresolved; extra fees, immediate discounts and travel are handled separately. Examples contain no live prices or personal information.
- Formula verification covers blank versus zero, adult/family arithmetic, all person/option positions, missing fees/doses/prices, invalid amounts, discounts, travel and cent precision. The XLSX was recalculated, rendered and exported/re-imported with the authoring spreadsheet engine. Native Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets interfaces were not tested.

This release includes only the public toolkit files. It does not change or publish the website's private application repository.
